# PRODUCT VISION

CampusPilot AI is an AI-powered digital campus platform that centralizes everything a student needs from the first day of college until graduation.

The application replaces scattered WhatsApp groups, PDFs, notice boards, spreadsheets, and multiple college portals with one unified intelligent platform.

Students should never need to ask questions like:

Where is the library?

Who teaches Data Structures?

When is CIA 2?

How do I register for clubs?

What events are happening today?

Where can I download previous year papers?

How do I apply for OD?

Everything should be accessible from one platform.

The system must look enterprise-grade.
